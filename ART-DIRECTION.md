# Tusho — Art Direction: "Plotter"

*For approval before any UI code. v1 — 2026-07-03*

## The idea

The whole site is drawn by one instrument. Your wordmark is built from thin architectural
strokes — so the site's design language is generated by it, not decorated with it. A single
1px line is born in the preloader, draws TUSHO stroke by stroke, and then never stops:
scroll is the pen, and the line travels the full length of the page — becoming every rule,
underline, frame, timeline, and the scroll-progress indicator along the way. The shell
behaves like a precision drawing in progress; your work (full-color, autoplaying) is the
only loud thing on the page.

Two other directions were generated and judged (an aircenter-faithful kinetic-typography
direction and a floating spatial-gallery direction). Plotter won 2 of 3 judging lenses:
it's the only direction that can't be transplanted to another portfolio — remove your
wordmark and the concept has nothing to draw. The best moves from the runner-up are
grafted in (see Animation language).

## The signature — THE SPINE

One continuous hairline that is never allowed to end:

preloader bounce → smears into a line → draws the wordmark → exits the counter of the
final O during the zoom reveal → hero baseline rule → turns 90° down a column edge →
divides the "What I do" rows → frames the first motion card, then releases the scatter →
extends under each count-up stat like a measurement → straightens into the
Listen → Research → Plan → Execute timeline → traces the About portrait frame →
underlines the footer email → terminates in a single filled dot. The pen lifts off the paper.

Its drawn length doubles as the scroll-progress indicator (a fixed 1px edge rule mirrors
percentage drawn). Built as per-section SVG/div segments with entry/exit points locked to
grid columns — reads as one unbroken path without being one fragile one.

## Type scale — Circular Std only

| Role | Weight | Size | Leading / tracking |
|------|--------|------|--------------------|
| Display (hero statements) | Black 900 | `clamp(4rem, 12vw, 12rem)` | 0.92 / −0.03em, sentence case |
| H2 (section titles) | Bold 700 | `clamp(2.5rem, 6.5vw, 6rem)` | 0.98 / −0.02em |
| H3 (card titles) | Medium 500 | `clamp(1.375rem, 2.4vw, 2rem)` | 1.1 / −0.01em |
| Body | Book 450 | `clamp(1rem, 0.95rem + 0.3vw, 1.125rem)` | 1.6 / 0, max-width 34rem |
| Label / annotation | Medium 500 | 0.75rem fixed | 1 / +0.14em, UPPERCASE |
| Stats | Black 900 | `clamp(3rem, 8vw, 7rem)` | 1, tabular-nums |

- Labels are the draftsman's voice — the only uppercase on the site, always paired with a rule or tick, like drawing annotations.
- Nothing between 2rem and display size: the scale jumps deliberately; hairlines bridge the gap.
- Book Italic reserved for one-line asides and the footer note.

## Color system — dark + light

Two roles, flipped by the toggle: `--ground` and `--stroke`.

| Token | Dark shell (default) | Light shell |
|-------|---------------------|-------------|
| `--ground` | Ink `#191A1B` | Paper `#FAFBFC` |
| `--stroke` | Paper `#FAFBFC` | Ink `#191A1B` |
| Resting hairlines | Charcoal `#38393A` | Ash `#CDD0D2` |
| Muted text / labels | Smoke `#848688` | Smoke `#848688` |

- The spine is always full-contrast `--stroke`. Resting structural lines sit at the muted
  tone and "ink up" to full contrast only when the pen activates them.
- No rare tone ever appears as a fill larger than a 2px stroke or a line of small text.
- Theme flip: 0.25s ease on background/color only; the spine redraws its visible segment
  as a 150ms flourish. Every dark-mode decision is mirrored in light mode, same session.
- Work media is the only color on the page — always inside a hard-cornered 1px frame,
  no border-radius, no shadows. In dark mode media renders at 96% brightness (kills glare),
  100% on hover.

## Layout grid

- **≥1080px:** 12 columns, 32px gutters, fluid margins `clamp(24px, 6vw, 120px)`, no
  max-width cap (body copy capped at 34rem instead).
- **768–1079px:** 8 columns, 24px gutters. **<768px:** 4 columns, 16px gutters.
- Vertical rhythm on an 8px base; section padding `clamp(96px, 18vh, 240px)` — one idea per viewport.
- The grid is drawn, not hidden: the spine always runs along column boundaries, so structure
  becomes visible exactly where the line goes. Headings snap to column 1, body starts at
  column 2, work cards scatter across columns 2–12 at mixed alignments (the "cloudy" drift
  stays loose but always lands on tracks).

## Animation language — five principles + two grafts

1. **Pen speed** — lines never fade; they draw, extend, retract at constant velocity
   (~600px/s, floor 150ms, cap 1.2s). Straight rules animate as `scaleX` on 1px divs
   (GPU-cheap); SVG `stroke-dashoffset` reserved for turns, the logo, and the spine.
2. **Rise and rule** — type enters only via SplitText line-masks (yPercent 110→0, expo.out,
   0.9s, stagger 0.06), always paired with a rule drawing beneath it. Type never fades, drifts, or scales.
3. **Straight paths only** — orthogonal or 45° vectors, max 120px offset. No rotation
   (except the preloader circle), no blur, no elastic, no bounce.
4. **Scrub grammar** — pinned scenes scrub with `ease: none` + 0.5s smoothing; released
   moments use expo/power families; hovers are 0.25s power2.out.
5. **What never moves** — color, letter-spacing, font-weight, body-text position.

**Grafts from the runner-up (judged in):**
- **Karaoke weight-snap** in exactly two places — hero pin and "How I think": as the spine
  sweeps a pinned statement, words behind it snap Book/Smoke → Black/full-contrast
  (stacked pre-rendered duplicates crossfaded on opacity — Circular Std isn't variable, so
  no reflow). This gives the pen a typographic voice and proves motion-type chops early.
- **Self-demonstrating "What I do" entrances** (the one sanctioned exception to straight
  paths): *Motion design* arrives as scattered characters snapping onto the freshly drawn
  rule; *Brand design* as a Book→Black weight crossfade settling onto its line; *Digital
  experiences* as a tracking collapse measured by the extending hairline.

**Degradation (designed, not amputated):**
- Device-tier gate (deviceMemory + quick rAF benchmark), offscreen tweens paused via
  IntersectionObserver — a weak laptop gets the mobile choreography at any width.
- Mobile <768px: no pins; spine collapses to per-section top rules; card drift off;
  SplitText hero-only.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: everything pre-drawn and static, 0.2s opacity-only reveals,
  preloader replaced by a 400ms wordmark hold.

## Section choreography (summary)

1. **Preloader** — your Lottie circle bounces; last contact smears into the hairline that draws T-U-S-H-O at pen speed; camera zooms through the counter of the O into the hero. *(Interim until your Lottie arrives: wordmark held on Ink field, line draws it, same zoom-through.)*
2. **Hero (pinned)** — Display Black statement rises from masks; spine idles beneath, drawing/retracting the wordmark's construction geometry; scrub strikes through statement one, rises statement two, then the spine turns 90° and leads out of the pin.
3. **What I do** — three full-width rows; hairline extends from spine to right margin, title rises above the line with its self-demonstrating entrance, description settles below in Smoke.
4. **Motion works** — spine draws the first card frame corner-to-corner, then releases: 6–8 cards fly in on straight vectors, frames self-drawing before video fades up; per-card drift speeds (0.85–1.15) give the cloudy parallax; uppercase coordinate labels like plotted figures.
5. **Brand works** — 4–5 calmer cards on alternating tracks; frame draws first, image wipes in via clip-path from the edge the pen finished on; a rule extends to exactly the card's edge, measuring it.
6. **Web projects** — ruled index rows, title left / stack right in Smoke; hover doubles the rule (draftsman emphasis) and slides a live thumbnail in along the row.
7. **Case studies** — pinned scenes; count-up stats in tabular Black while the spine extends beneath each number proportional to its value — metrics as drawn measurements; hairline-framed image expands to full bleed as the scrub completes.
8. **How I think (pinned)** — the spine straightens into a horizontal timeline; each scrub segment draws to the next station; Listen / Research / Plan / Execute rise at full contrast (karaoke snap) while the previous dims to Smoke, its segment staying inked.
9. **About + hobbies** — portrait frame draws, image wipes in, bio rises in Book; three single-stroke pictograms (motorcycle, football, drums) draw themselves left to right with annotations — the line language being playful without breaking discipline.
10. **Footer** — spine rules off the note box as a drawn rectangle, underlines the oversized email, stops at a filled dot: the pen lifts. Theme toggle is a line-drawn circle that fills to flip the world; the drawing re-inks in the opposite polarity in 250ms.

## Risks (named honestly)

- **Sterility** → violent scale contrast (12rem Black vs 1px strokes), full-color autoplaying work, each section gets a different line verb (extend / frame / measure / travel).
- **Spine fragility across breakpoints** → per-section segments locked to grid tracks, never one monolithic path; collapses to section rules on mobile.
- **Performance** → straight rules as transforms not SVG, ≤3 concurrent ScrollTriggers per viewport, `will-change` only while tweening, device-tier gate.
- **1px rendering on mixed DPI** → device-pixel borders for rules, `vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke` on all SVG.
- **Architect-portfolio cliché adjacency** → the unbroken spine with the pen-lift ending is the differentiator; it is protected first in any scope cut.
