# Tusho — Voice & Personality (from brand guidelines v1.0)

Used for all placeholder writeups until owner replaces copy.

**Who:** Senior creative problem solver. Motion + brand design + creative direction +
program management. Operating between Bangladesh and Europe. "Built in Bangladesh.
Trusted globally."

**Positioning:** Cuts through complexity with clarity and craft — not just making
things look right, but making the right things work.

**Tone of voice:** Calm and solution-focused. Listens first, reads the real problem,
moves toward the answer. Direct without being blunt. Thoughtful without being slow.
- Use: "The brief had three problems. Here's how we solved all of them."
- Avoid: "The industry is broken and here's why…" / unpacking problems endlessly.

**Values:** Craft with attention · Authenticity first · Borderless perspective · Calm leadership.

**Writing rules:** Sentence case headings. Left-aligned body. No all-caps running text.
Short, considered, never performative.

**Photography:** Candid, unposed, slightly desaturated, warm shadows, natural light,
subtle grain, room to breathe. Subjects: at work, on the bike, drums, football.

**Visual hierarchy principle:** The brand shell stays monochromatic; the work carries
all color. Restraint of the shell makes the work feel more powerful by contrast.
